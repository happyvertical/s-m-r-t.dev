# Product Guidelines: s-m-r-t Showcase & Documentation

## Prose & Tone

- **Style:** Clinical and sparse. The documentation should be highly technical with minimal fluff to ensure maximum clarity and speed of consumption.
- **Tone:** To-the-point with a very subtle dry wit.
- **Implementation of Wit:** Wit should be rare and integrated naturally, primarily through subtle, self-deprecating code comments within examples. Avoid overt jokes or distracting humor.
- **Clarity First:** Technical accuracy and brevity always take precedence over stylistic choices.

## Visual Identity & Branding

- **Design Aesthetic:** Clean "Swiss" Design. The site should utilize a rigorous grid-based layout, generous white space, and highly legible sans-serif typography. The overall feel should be professional, organized, and high-quality.
- **Brand Representation:** The brand name must always be presented as `s-m-r-t`.
- **Typography:** Use a monospace font for all instances of the `s-m-r-t` brand name to emphasize its technical and framework-oriented nature.
- **Color Palette:** Professional and understated, leaning into the Swiss design aesthetic (likely a neutral base with a single, clear accent color).

## Content Guidelines

- **Focus:** Application logic over implementation details. Highlight how `s-m-r-t` allows developers to focus on the "what" rather than the "how."
- **Code Examples:** Must be concise, idiomatic, and directly support the clinical prose style. Use `s-m-r-t` objects and declarations to demonstrate brevity.
- **FAQ:** Maintain the "to-the-point" style. The definitive answer to what `s-m-r-t` stands for is strictly the provided YouTube link, without further explanation.
